INT335 Design Thinking · P05 Waiting for the Shuttle

Next-Shuttle Countdown Board

A testing prototype. Open the Board on a laptop or tablet at the gate stop, and the Control panel on a teammate's phone to report shuttle departures.

Open Board Open Control
  1. Open the Board on the screen at the stop. It shows a 4-digit board code.
  2. Open Control on a phone, enter that code and press Connect.
  3. In Control → Settings, enter the travel time from the previous stop and the walking times you measured.
  4. When a shuttle leaves the previous stop, press Shuttle left. When it reaches the gate, press Arrived.
