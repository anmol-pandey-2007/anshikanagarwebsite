Test-day setup

  1. 1Open Board on a laptop or tablet at the stop. Note its 4-digit code.
  2. 2On the spotter's phone, open Control, enter the code, tap Connect.
  3. 3In Control → Settings, enter the travel time and walking times you measured.
  4. 4Tap Shuttle left when one departs and Arrived when it reaches the gate. Export the log for the report.
